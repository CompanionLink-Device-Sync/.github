## First Look at CompanionLink Professional

CompanionLink Professional is for people who keep Outlook and a device they own, and want one named contact bridge. It gives you a device sync, an Outlook desk, and a short path back to calendars you already use. It does not replace Outlook. It sits beside Outlook and keeps the pair close.
